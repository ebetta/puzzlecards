Card Rain – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy describes how the Card Rain app (Chuva de Cartas in Portuguese; package com.betta.puzzlecards), developed by Eduardo Betta, handles user information. It applies to every version of the game, in every language and country where it is distributed: the Android app, the web version and the version published on the CrazyGames portal. Differences between them are described below.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information in any version of the game. There is no account or login. The ads described below are provided by third parties, which handle their own data under their own policies.

Web version: no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. The game does not send any data over the internet.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob: a video you may choose to watch to get more Hint and Magic uses, and an ad between games (at most one every few minutes). To show and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time through the Ad choices link on the game's start screen. Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Version played on CrazyGames

When you play Card Rain on CrazyGames, the game runs inside the portal and uses the CrazyGames SDK, which shows ads (for example, a video you may choose to watch to get more Hint and Magic uses, and ads between games), saves your game progress to your CrazyGames account when you are logged in, and receives gameplay events (game loading, gameplay start and stop). This data is handled by CrazyGames and its advertising partners under the CrazyGames Privacy Policy, not by us.

Information stored on your device

The game stores only a few values: your current game number, your sound preference (on/off) and, in versions with ads, how many Hint and Magic uses you have left. In the Android app and the web version this data stays on your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. On CrazyGames it is saved by the portal, as described above.

Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, etc.). The Android app uses internet access and the advertising ID permission (AD_ID), both needed to show ads.

Children's privacy

Card Rain is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads in the Android app are provided by Google AdMob under Google's policies; on CrazyGames, ads and accounts follow the portal's own policies for young players.

Do Not Track

We do not track users over time or across third-party apps and websites. Ad providers (Google AdMob in the Android app, CrazyGames on its portal) follow their own policies; in the Android app you can limit ad personalization as described above.

Your rights

Since we do not hold any personal information about you, there is no data to access, correct, export or delete on our side. For data handled by Google or CrazyGames, use the controls and contacts described in their policies. Data stored on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. If you have questions about your privacy rights under the laws of your state or country (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR), contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before the change takes effect.
