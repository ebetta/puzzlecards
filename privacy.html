Card Rain – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy describes how the Card Rain app (Chuva de Cartas in Portuguese; package com.betta.puzzlecards), developed by Eduardo Betta, handles user information. It applies to every version of the game, in every language and country where it is distributed: the Android app, the web version and the version published on the CrazyGames portal. Differences between them are described below.

Information we collect

Card Rain does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. We, the developer, receive no data about you in any version of the game.

Android app and web version: no account or login, and no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. The game does not send any data over the internet.

Version played on CrazyGames

When you play Card Rain on CrazyGames, the game runs inside the portal and uses the CrazyGames SDK, which shows ads (for example, a video you may choose to watch to get more Hint and Magic uses, and ads between games), saves your game progress to your CrazyGames account when you are logged in, and receives gameplay events (game loading, gameplay start and stop). This data is handled by CrazyGames and its advertising partners under the CrazyGames Privacy Policy, not by us.

Information stored on your device

The game stores only a few values: your current game number, your sound preference (on/off) and, in versions with ads, how many Hint and Magic uses you have left. In the Android app and the web version this data stays on your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. On CrazyGames it is saved by the portal, as described above.

Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, etc.).

Children's privacy

Card Rain does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. In the Android app and the web version no data is collected, so there is nothing to share with third parties, consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). On CrazyGames, ads and accounts follow the portal's own policies for young players.

Do Not Track

The Android app and the web version do not track users over time or across third-party apps and websites, so they have no need to respond to Do Not Track signals. On CrazyGames, see the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Since we do not hold any personal information about you, there is no data to access, correct, export or delete on our side. For data handled by CrazyGames, contact CrazyGames as described in its policy. Data stored on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. If you have questions about your privacy rights under the laws of your state or country (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR), contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds ads or other features that use data — the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before the change takes effect.
