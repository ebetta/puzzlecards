Card Rain – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy describes how the Card Rain app (Chuva de Cartas in Portuguese; package com.betta.puzzlecards), developed by Eduardo Betta, handles user information. It applies to every version of the app, in every language and country where it is distributed.

Information we collect

Card Rain does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The game requires no account or login and contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. It works offline and does not send any data over the internet.

Information stored on your device

The game stores only two values locally on your device: your current game number and your sound preference (on/off). This data never leaves the device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, etc.).

Children's privacy

Card Rain does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties, consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Do Not Track

Because the app does not track users over time or across third-party apps and websites, it has no need to respond to Do Not Track signals.

Your rights

Since we do not hold any personal information about you, there is no data to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Data stored on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. If you have questions about your privacy rights under the laws of your state or country (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR), contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds ads or other features that use data — the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before the change takes effect.
